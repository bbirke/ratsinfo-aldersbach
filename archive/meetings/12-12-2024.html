Agenda

  1. TOP 01
    KenntnisnahmeSonstiges

    As part of the urban development measure „Brauereiumfeld mit Parkplatz“, the parking spaces at the southern east entrance were reorganised, requiring a gabion retaining wall and a railing as fall protection.

    The text states no explicit council decision; it only reports that the contract for the locksmith work was awarded to Firma MAW Weinberger GmbH in Vilshofen.

    Vilshofen

    Decision pageHighlighted PDF, p. 4Original PDF

    Originaltext (German, verbatim)
    Infos aus der Gemeinderatssitzung vom 12.12.2024 TOP 01 Im Zuge der Städtebaumaßnahme „Brauereiumfeld mit Parkplatz“ wurden die Parkplätze am südlichen Ostein- gang neu geordnet. Hierzu war die Errichtung einer Ga- bionenstützwand erforderlich. Um in diesem Bereich die Absturzsicherung zu gewährleisten, ist das Anbringen eines Geländers notwendig. Die Vergabe der Schlosser- arbeiten hierfür erfolgte an die Firma MAW Weinberger GmbH in 94474 Vilshofen.
  2. TOP 02
    KenntnisnahmeStraßen/Verkehr

    Award of landscaping works for the construction of a new footpath and cycle path along the ST 2108 between Freundorf and Uttigkofen, including a left-turn lane and pedestrian crossing aid.

    The council took note that the landscaping works were awarded to the firm Manfred Hechinger GbR of 94474 Vilshofen-Alkofen; no explicit decision verb is stated in the text.

  3. TOP 03
    KenntnisnahmeBauleitplanung

    The municipality of Aldersbach is involved as a public-interest body (Träger öffentlicher Belange) in the urban land-use planning (Bauleitplanung) of the city of Vilshofen concerning the amendment of the Bebauungsplan for the Gewerbegebiet „GE Linda“ by Deckblatt Nr. 13.

    The council took note that the municipality has no objections to the amendment of the Bebauungsplan „GE Linda“; no explicit decision verb is stated in the text.

    VertagungBauanträge

    The council considered a building application (Bauantrag) by Andreas Krause for the creation of an Ökokontofläche by the GreenneT Stiftung as part of the replacement construction of the Höchstspannungsleitung Pirach - Pleinting.

    The council did not reach a final decision and therefore adjourned ("vertagt") the decision.

  6. TOP 06
    BeschlussSonstiges

    The municipal council addressed the intercommunal funding program for vacancy activation (Leerstandsaktivierung) to stabilize and develop town centers from 2025.

    The council viewed the funding guidelines positively and approved ("beschließt") extending the funding program by two more years until 31.12.2026.

    Vote as printed: beschließt
