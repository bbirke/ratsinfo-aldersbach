About this archive

A citizen asks: what was decided about X, when, and how did it develop? This archive answers in English, with the verbatim German source one click away.

What it is

An independent, non-official archive of the decisions of the Gemeinderat Aldersbach (Landkreis Passau, Bavaria), covering 20 meetings from 12.12.2024 to 02.09.2026: 215 agenda items, 60 documents and 321 map locations.

Sources

The primary source is the section "Infos aus der Gemeinderatssitzung" in the quarterly municipal newsletter Aldersbacher Klosterkurier. Older issues were recovered from the Wayback Machine, current ones from the municipal website and the Heimat-Info platform.

How it was made

  • Text and page geometry were extracted from the PDFs; advertisements and layout noise were removed by geometric rules.
  • For each agenda item a local language model wrote the English subject and decision, a topic tag and the facts, derived only from the German source text, never from another summary.
  • Every quote and every fact was then checked literally against the extracted page text. All 215 items pass.
  • Decision-kind badges come from the printed vote phrase ("beschloss", "stimmte zu", "Kenntnisnahme", ...). See the glossary.
  • Place names printed in the text were resolved via OpenStreetMap; parcel numbers (Flurstücke) via BayernAtlas (ALKIS). Approximate pins are marked with *.
  • The highlighted PDF copies mark each cited passage in yellow; the original PDFs are kept untouched next to them.

Known gaps

  • The newsletter prints summaries, not minutes. Votes appear only when the text names them; vote counts are never given.
  • Meetings before 12.12.2024 are not covered.
  • Some TOP numbers are missing in print (29.01.2025 jumps from TOP 14 to 16; 25.03.2026 reports only TOP 01 to 06).
  • Klosterkurier 4/2026 is announced for 10.12.2026 and not yet included.

Official council information system

On 29.07.2026 the council decided to introduce the AKDB "Session.Net" council information system. It was not online when this archive was built. Once it is, this archive can switch to the official agendas and minutes.
