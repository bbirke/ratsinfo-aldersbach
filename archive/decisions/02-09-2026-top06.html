TOP 06Council meeting Wednesday, 2 September 2026

The council considered a request by Manuela Haase for a preliminary building decision (Vorbescheid) for the demolition of an existing building in Kriestorf, deemed not suitable for renovation, and the construction of a replacement building adapted to the neighbouring development.

Billigung · what was decided

The council approved ("billigt") the application for a Vorbescheid for the demolition and replacement building in Kriestorf.
