TOP 12Council meeting Thursday, 11 December 2025

Recalculation of the connection fees (Herstellungsbeiträge) for the municipal water supply, as required by the Rechnungsprüfungsamt, with new rates per square metre of Grundstücksfläche and Geschoßfläche.

Kenntnisnahme · what was decided

The council set new water supply fee rates (0.88 € per m² Grundstücksfläche and 6.81 € per m² Geschoßfläche), effective from 01.01.2026; however, the text states no explicit decision verb, so no formal decision is recorded.

Decision kind
Kenntnisnahme taken note, no decision
