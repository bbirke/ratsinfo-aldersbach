TOP 16Council meeting Wednesday, 16 April 2025

Entlastung (discharge) of the municipal administration regarding the annual accounts of the Gemeinde Aldersbach for the fiscal year 2024.

Beschluss · what was decided

The council granted discharge ("Entlastung erteilt") for the established result of the 2024 annual accounts.

Previous item, TOP 15The item concerns the annual accounts (Jahresrechnung) of the municipality of Aldersbach for the budget year 2024.Next item, TOP 17Confirmation of Sebastian Hantke as first commander (erster Kommandant) of the Freiwillige Feuerwehr Pörndorf.

Same meeting, 16.04.2025

  • TOP 01Announcement of the purchase of roller shutters and a roof-mounted awning for shading at the Kindergarten St. Maria in Aldersbach.
  • TOP 02Announcement of contract awards for the renovation of the north facade with canopy (Vordach) of the Brauereigebäude, covering scaffolding, window and metalwork works.
  • TOP 03The council addressed the official application procedure for the designation of ground-mounted photovoltaic (Freiflächenphotovoltaikanlagen) sites, with applications from Beck (15 MWp) in Neustift and Naneder (500 KWp) in Wetzstein, and the commissioning of landscape architect Frau Jocham as site selection advisor.
  • TOP 04-05E.ON plans to build a photovoltaic facility in Edelsbrunn in Aldersbach, with the architecture office „JOCHAM KESSLER KELLHUBER“ from Iggensbach having drafted the project-related development plan (vorhabenbezogener Bebauungsplan) „So Photovoltaikpark Edelsbrunn“ and the amendment of the land-use plan (Flächennutzungsplan) via Deckblatt Nr. 36.
  • TOP 06Amendment of the wall-height provisions within the „Bebauungsplan GE/GI Aldersbach Nord“ (development plan for the industrial and commercial area) in connection with expansion works of Knorr-Bremse SfN GmbH.
  • TOP 07The council considered a building application (Bauantrag) from Knorr-Bremse SfN GmbH for the extension of an existing rainwater retention basin (Regenrückhaltebecken) in Aldersbach.
  • TOP 08The planned expansion of Knorr-Bremse SfN GmbH, including the construction of new halls, within the „Bebauungsplanes Gewerbe- und Industriegebiet Aldersbach-Nord“ (development plan for the commercial and industrial area Aldersbach-Nord) in Aldersbach.
  • TOP 09-10Building applications (Bauanträge) in Pörndorf and Walchsing: a vehicle hall by Friedrich Klattenbacher and the demolition and reconstruction of an existing single-family house by Stefan Hermannseder.
  • TOP 11The council considered a building application (Bauantrag) by Siegfried Maier for the construction of a fifth garage in Adenberg.
  • TOP 12Building application by Bettina Fellner for the construction of a replacement residential house with carport in Hinteröd.
  • TOP 13-14The council considered applications for preliminary building decisions (Vorbescheid): Daniela Weidlich's construction of an operating manager's residence with garage in Neustift and Christoph Schramm's construction of a single-family house in Walchsing.
  • TOP 15The item concerns the annual accounts (Jahresrechnung) of the municipality of Aldersbach for the budget year 2024.
  • TOP 16Entlastung (discharge) of the municipal administration regarding the annual accounts of the Gemeinde Aldersbach for the fiscal year 2024.
  • TOP 17Confirmation of Sebastian Hantke as first commander (erster Kommandant) of the Freiwillige Feuerwehr Pörndorf.
  • TOP 18Confirmation of Markus Konrad as deputy commander (stellvertretender Kommandant) of the Freiwillige Feuerwehr Pörndorf.
  • TOP 19The municipal council considered a funding request from the Tennisclub Aldersbach e. V. for the general renovation of the sprinkler system (Sprenkleranlage) at the tennis facility.
