TOP 07Council meeting Thursday, 12 December 2024

The council dealt with the annual accounts (Jahresrechnung) for 2023 submitted by the Caritasverband für die Diözese Passau e. V. for the Kindergarten St. Maria in Aldersbach.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council approved ("stimmte zu") the 2023 annual accounts of the Caritasverband für die Diözese Passau e. V. for the Kindergarten St. Maria.
