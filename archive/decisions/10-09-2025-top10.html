TOP 10Council meeting Wednesday, 10 September 2025

The TC Kriestorf e. V. applied by letter dated 25.08.2025 for a grant (Zuschuss) toward the purchase of a new lawn mower.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council granted ("gewährt") a one-time subsidy of 30 % of the acquisition costs in accordance with the applicable funding guidelines for grants to clubs.
