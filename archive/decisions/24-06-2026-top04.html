TOP 04Council meeting Wednesday, 24 June 2026

The council considered a grant under the „Interkommunales Förderprogrammes Leerstandaktivierung" of the ILE Klosterwinkel for the owner of a property in Walchsing, including a required renovation and construction consultation (Sanierungs- und Bauberatung).

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council consented ("Zustimmung") to a subsidy of 10.000, - € plus 5 hours of Bauberatung for the applicant and property owner in Walchsing.
