TOP 12Council meeting Wednesday, 25 February 2026

Authorization for the mayor to sign a purpose agreement (Zweckvereinbarung) between the municipality of Aldersbach and the Landkreis Passau regarding the joint appointment of a data protection officer for the district and its member municipalities.

Beschluss · what was decided

The council empowered the mayor to sign the Zweckvereinbarung with the Landkreis Passau concerning the joint data protection officer.

Previous item, TOP 11The council considered a proposal by the Freiwillige Feuerwehr Walchsing to establish Kinderfeuerwehren (children's fire brigades) in the municipality of Aldersbach.Next item, TOP 13-14The council considered two building applications (Bauanträge) in Walchsing: a change of commercial use on the ground floor to an apartment (Josefa Resch) and the construction of a new residential house with carport (Christoph Schramm).

Same meeting, 25.02.2026

  • TOP 1The council considered the creation of a new municipal homepage for the Gemeinde Aldersbach, as the existing homepage no longer meets current security standards and legal requirements. The award of the services „Erstellung und Neugestaltung“ (creation and redesign) was discussed.
  • TOP 2Presentation of the accountability report (Rechenschaftsbericht) for the 2025 annual financial statement to the municipal council.
  • TOP 3The council addressed verkaufsoffene Sonntage (Sundays with permitted shop opening hours) in 2026, specifically the release of 23.08.2026 for the DiGA Gartenmesse.
  • TOP 4Two applications from the municipality of Aldersbach under the Regionalbudget of the ILE Klosterwinkel: the purchase of a used all-season ice rink and the project „Klostergeschichte Aldersbach erlebbar machen“.
  • TOP 5The council considered an application by DJK Pörndorf e.V. for a subsidy to build a well for irrigating its sports grounds and for milling work to lay the supply pipes.
  • TOP 6The FC Aldersbach e.V. applied for a municipal subsidy for the renovation of its alternative sports ground (Ausweichplatz), partial renovation of the club house, and marking paint.
  • TOP 7The item concerns an application by the Kreisgruppe Vilshofen for permission to set up and operate a container for a Konfiskatkühlung (confiscated-material cooling) at the Kläranlage (wastewater treatment plant) in Aldersbach.
  • TOP 8The council dealt with new dedication orders (Widmungsverfügungen) for the municipal road „Stocköder Straße“ and a public field and forest path of the same name in the Gemarkung Aldersbach.
  • TOP 9The council dealt with the reclassification (Widmungsverfügung) of the former Staatsstraße as the municipal road „Emmersdorfer Straße“ in the Gemarkung Haidenburg.
  • TOP 10The municipality of Aldersbach was involved by the city of Vilshofen in the preparation of the Außenbereichssatzung (outdoor-area statute) 'Mühldorferöd'.
  • TOP 11The council considered a proposal by the Freiwillige Feuerwehr Walchsing to establish Kinderfeuerwehren (children's fire brigades) in the municipality of Aldersbach.
  • TOP 12Authorization for the mayor to sign a purpose agreement (Zweckvereinbarung) between the municipality of Aldersbach and the Landkreis Passau regarding the joint appointment of a data protection officer for the district and its member municipalities.
  • TOP 13-14The council considered two building applications (Bauanträge) in Walchsing: a change of commercial use on the ground floor to an apartment (Josefa Resch) and the construction of a new residential house with carport (Christoph Schramm).
  • TOP 15Notification of building projects exempt from approval procedures (Genehmigungsfreistellungsverfahren): a battery storage facility by Photo Volta Batteriespeicher Aldersbach GmbH in the Bebauungsplan „SO Energie- und Batteriespeicheranlage am Umspannwerk“ and a production hall by HU-Immo GmbH in the Bebauungsplan „Gewerbegebiet Uttigkofen“.

Same places

  • 02.09.2026Award of the landscaping (Grünordnung) works for the doctors' house (Ärztehaus) connecting to the Waldschulsiedlung area near Ritter-Tuschl-Str., within the urban development funding (Städtebauförderung) of Aldersbach.
  • 02.09.2026Replacement of the existing entrance gate at the Kläranlage Aldersbach (wastewater treatment plant) due to age, technical defects and TÜV inspection complaints.
  • 02.09.2026Presentation and approval of the draft development plan (Bebauungsplan) for the new building area „Schwaig X“, including the completed traffic study on the Abt-Zanker-Straße and the Erschließungskonzept.
  • 02.09.2026The council considered a request from the Nachbarschaftshilfe Aldersbach and the Helferkreis Aidenbach for increased financial support of the non-profit facility, including rent costs for the „Repair Cafe“ and „Kleiderkammer“.
  • 02.09.2026The council considered an application by TC Aldersbach e. V. for a subsidy to convert the four existing Ziegelmehlplätze (clay courts) into year-round all-weather courts, with a total investment volume of around 300,000 Euro.
  • 02.09.2026Due to recurring vandalism incidents in Aldersbach, the council considered the introduction of a Sicherheitswacht (volunteer security watch) to support the police.
  • 29.07.2026The municipality of Aldersbach is having its homepage completely renewed due to outdated security standards and legal accessibility requirements, awarded to the Cosmema GmbH from Gaimersheim, including a new structure, updated content, social media and app integration, and an AI-based virtual town hall assistant.
  • 29.07.2026The building application (Bauantrag) by Josef Buchbauer for the construction of two commercial halls (Gewerbehallen) in Aldersbach.
