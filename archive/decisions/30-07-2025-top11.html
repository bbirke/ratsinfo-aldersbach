TOP 11Council meeting Wednesday, 30 July 2025

The council dealt with a cost reimbursement agreement (Kostenübernahmevereinbarung) with E.ON Business Solutions Deutschland GmbH regarding the Bebauungsplan ‚SO Photovoltaikpark Edelsbrunn', under which the applicant bears the planning costs.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council approved ("stimmte zu") the agreement between the Gemeinde Aldersbach and E.ON Business Solutions Deutschland GmbH and commissioned Mayor Mayrhofer to sign it.
