TOP 03Council meeting Wednesday, 29 April 2026

The council dealt with a Freiflächenphotovoltaikanlagen (ground-mounted solar) application, specifically an Agri-PV-Zaunanlage project, after the applicant withdrew the planned Freiflächenanlage variant.

Ablehnung · what was decided

The council rejected ("abgelehnt") the Agri-PV-Zaunanlage after review by landscape architect Frau Jocham, citing failure to meet the minimum distance to existing residential development and possible settlement expansion as well as impairment of the local townscape.

Same meeting, 29.04.2026

  • TOP 01The council discussed the redesign of the eastern village entrance (BA III Baulos), including a footpath connection to the Ärztehaus via a footbridge structure to the Ritter-Tuschl-Straße.
  • TOP 02The municipal council awarded the steel construction contract (Stahlbau) for a measure funded under the Städtebauförderung to the firm Pichler GmbH, following the recommendation of the engineering office Schönbuchner.
  • TOP 03The council dealt with a Freiflächenphotovoltaikanlagen (ground-mounted solar) application, specifically an Agri-PV-Zaunanlage project, after the applicant withdrew the planned Freiflächenanlage variant.
  • TOP 04Beratung und Beschlussfassung über den Haushaltsplan 2026 und die Haushaltssatzung 2026 (municipal budget plan and budget statute 2026).

  • TOP 07Revision of the municipal subsidy guidelines for associations (Zuschussrichtlinien) and congratulation rules, as well as adjustment of the funding guidelines „Familienfreundliche Gemeinde“ (family-friendly municipality) of Aldersbach.
  • TOP 08The DJK Pörndorf e. V. applied for a subsidy for the purchase of new table tennis tables.
  • TOP 09-10The council considered building pre-application requests (Anträge auf Vorbescheid), including a new fire station for the Feuerwehr Uttigkofen-Haidenburg and a new storage hall in Uttigkofen.
