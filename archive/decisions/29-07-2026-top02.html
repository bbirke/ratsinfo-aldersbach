TOP 02Council meeting Wednesday, 29 July 2026

Replacement of the outdated remote control technology at the Drucksteigerungspumpwerk Wifling (booster pump station), which caused repeated failures and data transmission faults to the municipal water supply's process control system.

Beschluss · what was decided

The council approved ("beschloss") awarding the delivery, installation and commissioning of the new components to Elektrotechnik Paintner GmbH for a total price of 8.084€ net.

Same meeting, 29.07.2026

  • TOP 01Award of a contract to AKDB for the Ratsinformationssystem add-on module „Session.Net“ (council information system), which complements the existing meeting management software „Session“.
  • TOP 02Replacement of the outdated remote control technology at the Drucksteigerungspumpwerk Wifling (booster pump station), which caused repeated failures and data transmission faults to the municipal water supply's process control system.
  • TOP 03Award of planning services for the municipal heat planning (kommunale Wärmeplanung) in a simplified procedure to the Institut für Energietechnik (IfE) GmbH in Amberg.
  • TOP 04The council dealt with the planning design for the second construction phase of the renovation of the outdoor facilities at the Grundschule Aldersbach, covering the area between the Turnhalle and the Verkehrsübungsplatz.
  • TOP 05The council addressed the adjustment of the existing property insurance (Sachversicherung) for the buildings of the Brauerei, the Kloster and the Schützenheim.
  • TOP 06The municipality of Aldersbach is having its homepage completely renewed due to outdated security standards and legal accessibility requirements, awarded to the Cosmema GmbH from Gaimersheim, including a new structure, updated content, social media and app integration, and an AI-based virtual town hall assistant.
  • TOP 07-08The council considered two applications for a preliminary building decision (Vorbescheid): Katja Bachhuber's new single-family home with ground and upper floor in Gumperting, and Franz Straubinger's carport for the attic apartment near the municipal border with Aidenbach.
  • TOP 09The council addressed a building application (Bauantrag) from Rudolf Kalhamer for the construction of a new single-family house with double garage as a replacement building (Ersatzbau) in Freundorf.
  • TOP 10The building application (Bauantrag) by Josef Buchbauer for the construction of two commercial halls (Gewerbehallen) in Aldersbach.
  • TOP 11The council addressed the initiation of a Bebauungs- und Grünordnungsplan (development and green structure plan) for the area Haidenburg/Häusling, including a general residential area and a special area for a Schützenheim (marksmen's clubhouse).
  • TOP 12The council addressed the conclusion of a Sondernutzungsvertrag (special use agreement) with the TenneT TSO GmbH regarding the planned replacement construction of the 380-kV overhead line between Pirach and Pleinting, which requires the use of several municipal roads beyond normal public use during the construction phase.
  • TOP 13The council considered the request of the Caritasverband für die Diözese Passau e. V. for financial contribution for 2026 to 2031 to continue the Pädagogisch-Psychologischer Dienst (PPD) as a kindergarten specialist service for children with special needs.
  • TOP 14The DJK Pörndorf e. V. applied for a subsidy for the purchase of new football goals at the Hauptplatz.
  • TOP 15The council considered the building application (Bauantrag) of Dr. Nikolaus Arndt for the construction of a senior-friendly residential facility in Aldersbach.
  • TOP 16The council reviewed the revised preliminary design (Vorentwurf) for the second construction phase of the urban redevelopment measure "Zisterzienser Straße mit Umfeld", covering the renovation of the Zisterzienser Straße, access to the Waldspielplatz, parking improvements, better access to the Kloster, Klostergarten and Biergarten, and upgrading of the Spielplatz am Hang.
