TOP 06-07Council meeting Wednesday, 19 February 2025

The council considered an application to establish a vorhabenbezogener Bebauungsplan (project-related development plan) with a Flächennutzungsplan amendment for the construction of an Agri-PV-Anlage (agricultural photovoltaic installation) in Wetzstein.

Beschluss · what was decided

The council approved ("beschließt") to draw up the Bebauungs- und Grünordnungsplan „SO Photovoltaikpark Wetzstein“ and to amend the Flächennutzungsplan for the area.
