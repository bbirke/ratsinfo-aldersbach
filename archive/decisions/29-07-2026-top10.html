TOP 10Council meeting Wednesday, 29 July 2026

The building application (Bauantrag) by Josef Buchbauer for the construction of two commercial halls (Gewerbehallen) in Aldersbach.

Vertagung · what was decided

The council postponed ("vertagt") the decision until complete documents, especially the traffic and development concept, are submitted.
