TOP 08Council meeting Wednesday, 2 September 2026

The council considered an application by TC Aldersbach e. V. for a subsidy to convert the four existing Ziegelmehlplätze (clay courts) into year-round all-weather courts, with a total investment volume of around 300,000 Euro.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council approved ("zugestimmt") the subsidy application in accordance with the guidelines for granting subsidies to clubs.
