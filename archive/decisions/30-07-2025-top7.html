TOP 7Council meeting Wednesday, 30 July 2025

The council addressed securing building rights for a new fire station (Feuerwehrhaus) for the Feuerwehr Uttigkofen-Haidenburg by means of a preliminary building inquiry (Bauvoranfrage).

Beschluss · what was decided

The council approved ("beschloss") submitting a Bauvoranfrage for two possible land parcel variants to the Landratsamt Passau, with the relevant specialist authorities involved.

Previous item, TOP 03-06The council considered several building applications (Bauanträge), including a new powder-coating hall with office and storage space by Metak GmbH in Aldersbach, a winter garden extension in Uttigkofen, and two lightweight halls in Aldersbach.Next item, TOP 08The municipality received notice from the City of Vilshofen that its city council resolved an amendment to the Bebauungsplan „Vilsfeld“ by means of Deckblatt Nr. 68.

Same meeting, 30.07.2025

  • TOP 01Renewal of the stormwater sewer in Uttigkofen along the Schartnergasse to the Eggersdorfer Straße up to the Sulzbach, after camera inspection revealed leaks at several points.
  • TOP 02Introduction of the split wastewater fee („gesplittete Abwassergebühr“) in Aldersbach, requiring a new software module and support from Ingenieurbüro S² for the transition.
  • TOP 03-06The council considered several building applications (Bauanträge), including a new powder-coating hall with office and storage space by Metak GmbH in Aldersbach, a winter garden extension in Uttigkofen, and two lightweight halls in Aldersbach.
  • TOP 7The council addressed securing building rights for a new fire station (Feuerwehrhaus) for the Feuerwehr Uttigkofen-Haidenburg by means of a preliminary building inquiry (Bauvoranfrage).
  • TOP 08The municipality received notice from the City of Vilshofen that its city council resolved an amendment to the Bebauungsplan „Vilsfeld“ by means of Deckblatt Nr. 68.
  • TOP 09The council dealt with the planned photovoltaic park by E.ON in Edelsbrunn ('SO Photovoltaikpark Edelsbrunn') and the preparation of the vorhabenbezogener Bebauungsplan (project-related development plan), including citizen participation and input from specialist authorities.
  • TOP 10Early public participation (frühzeitige Beteiligung) for the amendment of the Flächennutzungs- und Landschaftsplan, carried out in parallel with the Bebauungsplan procedure.
  • TOP 11The council dealt with a cost reimbursement agreement (Kostenübernahmevereinbarung) with E.ON Business Solutions Deutschland GmbH regarding the Bebauungsplan ‚SO Photovoltaikpark Edelsbrunn', under which the applicant bears the planning costs.
  • TOP 12Notification that the Anhörungsverfahren (hearing procedure) for the Planfeststellungsverfahren (plan approval procedure) for the new 380-kV power line Prienbach - Pleinting by TenneT TSO GmbH has been initiated, including the route options „Trasse 2A“ and „Trasse 2B“.
  • TOP 13The council dealt with broadband (Glasfaserausbau) expansion in Aldersbach, where a market exploration procedure from 15 April to 13 June 2025 found no net provider willing to expand fiber commercially. The municipality will submit a funding application under the federal Gigabit funding directive and, if approved, conduct a selection procedure for a network operator.

Same places
