TOP 6Council meeting Wednesday, 10 September 2025

Due to the abolition of the statutory parking space requirement in the Bavarian Building Code (Bayerische Bauordnung) as of 01.10.2025, the municipality of Aldersbach is issuing a Stellplatzsatzung (parking space bylaw) oriented on the previous regulation.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council consented ("Zustimmung des Gemeinderats") to the issuance of the Stellplatzsatzung.
