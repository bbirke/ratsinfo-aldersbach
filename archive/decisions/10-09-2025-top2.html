TOP 2Council meeting Wednesday, 10 September 2025

Procurement of a small New Holland municipal tractor (with snow plough, winter spreader and flail mulcher) to replace the old lawn mower tractor.

Kenntnisnahme · what was decided

The council decided to award the purchase to the firm Hupfloher; the text states the Vergabe (award) occurs to this firm but names no explicit decision verb.

Decision kind
Kenntnisnahme taken note, no decision
Topic

Same meeting, 10.09.2025

  • TOP 01The council decided on the purchase of a New Holland tractor with snow plow and spreading device as a replacement for the old loader.
  • TOP 2Procurement of a small New Holland municipal tractor (with snow plough, winter spreader and flail mulcher) to replace the old lawn mower tractor.
  • TOP 3Erection of a digital information and advertising display at the Brauereiparkplatz as part of urban development funding (Städtebauförderung), with the contract awarded to the firm Rotapanel (Netherlands).
  • TOP 4The municipal council considered the tender for the road rehabilitation works planned for 2025, prepared by Ingenieurbüro Straubinger based on inspections by the engineer, administration and Bauhof.
  • TOP 5Replacement of the two old fire station gates of the Freiwillige Feuerwehr Pörndorf with modern electric sectional doors, awarded to Firma Kasberger from Passau.
  • TOP 6Due to the abolition of the statutory parking space requirement in the Bavarian Building Code (Bayerische Bauordnung) as of 01.10.2025, the municipality of Aldersbach is issuing a Stellplatzsatzung (parking space bylaw) oriented on the previous regulation.
  • TOP 7The Regional Planning Association (Regionale Planungsverband) has designated a priority area for wind energy installations (Vorranggebiet für Windenergieanlagen) east of Haidenburg.
  • TOP 8The module planning of the PV-Park Edelsbrunn was changed from east-west to south orientation, with adjustments to module height, tilt and the glare assessment (Blendgutachten).
  • TOP 9The council discussed the suitability of five inner-village sites in Aldersbach for a senior housing project („Seniorenwohnen“) examined by the commissioned architecture firm Arc.
  • TOP 10The TC Kriestorf e. V. applied by letter dated 25.08.2025 for a grant (Zuschuss) toward the purchase of a new lawn mower.
  • TOP 11The Freiwillige Feuerwehr Uttigkofen-Haidenburg applied for a subsidy for a Tandem-Tieflade-Kofferanhänger (tandem low-loader box trailer). The council considered the grant request.
