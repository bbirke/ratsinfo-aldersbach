TOP 07Council meeting Wednesday, 24 June 2026

Presentation by the senior citizens' representatives (Seniorenbeauftragte) Heidy and Dieter Rabs on the senior citizens' work carried out in the municipality of Aldersbach, using a PowerPoint presentation.

Kenntnisnahme · what was decided

No decision was made; the council received an informational presentation, and Mayor Mayrhofer thanked the senior citizens' representatives for their highly committed volunteer work.

Kenntnisnahme taken note, no decision

Same meeting, 24.06.2026

  • TOP 01Announcement of the renovation of the sanitary facilities at the Kindergarten Walchsing, including the award of planning services to the planning office Riedl from Passau.
  • TOP 02Due to objections from residents regarding the development of the Baugebiet „Schwaig X“, the administration, together with the engineering office, obtained a quote for a traffic study (Verkehrsuntersuchung) covering the Abt-Zanker-Straße and the Baugebiet Schwaig X.
  • TOP 03Introduction of the Heimat-Info App for the municipality of Aldersbach as a supplement to the homepage, including free public relations opportunities for local clubs.
  • TOP 04The council considered a grant under the „Interkommunales Förderprogrammes Leerstandaktivierung" of the ILE Klosterwinkel for the owner of a property in Walchsing, including a required renovation and construction consultation (Sanierungs- und Bauberatung).
  • TOP 05Award of the Bürgermedaille in Bronze (citizens' medal in bronze) to four honorees for their outstanding voluntary, communal-political and social merits.
  • TOP 06The council considered the award of the Bürgermedaille in Gold (citizens' medal in gold) for outstanding voluntary and civic service.
  • TOP 07Presentation by the senior citizens' representatives (Seniorenbeauftragte) Heidy and Dieter Rabs on the senior citizens' work carried out in the municipality of Aldersbach, using a PowerPoint presentation.
  • TOP 08The council dealt with an urban development contract (städtebaulicher Vertrag) between the municipality of Aldersbach and Marion Wollner from Aidenbach concerning cost coverage for a possibly required left-turn lane and resulting planning costs.
  • TOP 09Amendment of the outdated Bebauungsplan (land-use plan) "SO Golf" due to a minor overlap with a nature-conservation compensation area, based on a draft cover sheet by Planungsbüro Straubinger.
  • TOP 10-11The council considered two building applications (Bauanträge): Fabian Wenger's construction of a new storage hall in Walchsing and Armin Bauer's reconstruction of agricultural outbuildings after fire damage at the Anwesen Vogler property.
  • TOP 12The council dealt with the reclassification (Widmung) of the former Kreisstraße from the edge of Gainstorf to the municipal boundary as a Gemeindeverbindungsstraße following a retroactive land register correction (Grundbuchberichtigung).
  • TOP 13The tennis club TC Kriestorf applied for a subsidy for the purchase of a new submersible pump at the tennis home (Tennisheim).
  • TOP 14The item concerns the wastewater treatment plant (Kläranlage) Aldersbach and the second amendment of the Zweckvereinbarung (mutual agreement) between the Gemeinde Aldersbach and the Markt Aidenbach on joint wastewater disposal, including an adjustment of the cost allocation key for operating, maintenance and investment costs.
