TOP 04Council meeting Monday, 19 May 2025

The council dealt with the amendment of the Bebauungsplan 'Warbachweg' (development plan) of the Stadt Vilshofen an der Donau.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council approved ("stimmte zu") the amendment of the Bebauungsplan 'Warbachweg'.

Same meeting, 19.05.2025

  • TOP 01The council dealt with the 2025 municipal budget plan (Haushaltsplan 2025) and the 2025 budget statute (Haushaltssatzung 2025).
  • TOP 02The council addressed the Finanzplan mit Investitionsprogramm 2024 - 2028 (financial plan with investment program 2024 - 2028).
  • TOP 03The municipality of Aldersbach was involved in the land-use planning (Bauleitplanung) of a battery storage facility for renewable energies in the municipality of Roßbach, concerning the planned amendment of the development plan "Industrie- und Gewerbegebiet Esterndorf".
  • TOP 04The council dealt with the amendment of the Bebauungsplan 'Warbachweg' (development plan) of the Stadt Vilshofen an der Donau.
  • TOP 05-06Aufstellung des vorhabenbezogenen Bebauungs- und Grünordnungsplans „SO Photovoltaikpark Neustift“ in der Gemarkung Walchsing, einschließlich einer Änderung des Flächennutzungsplans.
  • TOP 07The council considered a building application (Bauantrag) by Rainer Freundorfer for the construction of a single-family house with garage in Seier.
  • TOP 08Presentation of a feasibility study by the architecture firm Arc Architekten on finding a possible location for the Feuerwehr Uttigkofen - Haidenburg and the Schützenverein Haidenburg, considering a new fire station in Uttigkofen and conversion of the old fire station in Haidenburg into a Schützenheim.

Same places

  • 16.10.2025The municipality of Aldersbach was involved in the public consultation on the amendment of the Flächennutzungsplan (land use plan) by Deckblatt Nr. 93 and the establishment of the Bebauungsplan „WA am Zeitlarner Berg II“ of the city of Vilshofen an der Donau.
  • 25.06.2025The municipality of Aldersbach is formally participating in changes to the Bebauungsplan „Unterfeld“ (Deckblatt Nr. 80) and the Bebauungsplan „Lindahof III“ (Deckblatt Nr. 1), both carried out by the Stadt Vilshofen an der Donau.
