TOP 12-16Council meeting Wednesday, 25 June 2025

The municipal council dealt with several building applications (Bauanträge), including replacement agricultural hall in Reit, new greenhouses in Wifling, and new residential and multi-purpose halls in Aldersbach and Pörndorf.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council approved ("zugestimmt") the listed building applications.

Same meeting, 25.06.2025

  • TOP 01The council discussed a site investigation (Standortuntersuchung) for senior housing (Seniorenwohnen) in the municipality of Aldersbach, with 5 possible sites identified.
  • TOP 02Adjustment of the awarded contract for roller shutters and an on-roof awning for shading at the Kindergarten St. Maria in Aldersbach, as the original contract was no longer the most economical offer.
  • TOP 03Award of planning services for a traffic-calming road narrowing in the Dietmar-von-Ayst-Straße (near Schaltbau), including relocation of a fire water hydrant and renewal of the water main.
  • TOP 04The municipal council awarded the planning services for the 2025 road rehabilitation works (Straßensanierungsarbeiten 2025) in the municipal area to the engineering office Straubinger.
  • TOP 05Changes in the development (Erschließung) of the building area „Schwaig X“ require a revision of the Bebauungsplan, including public infiltration swales for rainwater disposal.
  • TOP 06The company BRG Donau-Wald GmbH & Co. KG applied for a modification permit (Änderungsgenehmigung) under the Federal Immission Control Act (Bundesimmissionsschutzgesetz) to operate a construction rubble recycling plant at the landfill site Deponie-Standort Hiendlöd.
  • TOP 07-08The council dealt with the vorhabenbezogener Bebauungsplan (project-related development plan) „SO Energie - Batteriespeicheranlage am Umspannwerk“ for a battery storage facility in Pörndorf, including the parallel amendment of the Flächennutzungs- und Landschaftsplan by Deckblatt Nr. 35.
  • TOP 09The company Knorr-Bremse SfN GmbH applied for the immission control partial permit I (immissionsschutzrechtliche Teilgenehmigung I) for a major modification of its surface treatment facility, including a new KTL plant with zinc phosphating and a new anodizing plant. The municipality of Aldersbach is again participating in the further procedure.
  • TOP 10-11The municipality of Aldersbach is formally participating in changes to the Bebauungsplan „Unterfeld“ (Deckblatt Nr. 80) and the Bebauungsplan „Lindahof III“ (Deckblatt Nr. 1), both carried out by the Stadt Vilshofen an der Donau.
  • TOP 12-16The municipal council dealt with several building applications (Bauanträge), including replacement agricultural hall in Reit, new greenhouses in Wifling, and new residential and multi-purpose halls in Aldersbach and Pörndorf.
  • TOP 17The FC Aldersbach e. V. applied for a subsidy for the purchase of a goal and a spreader.

Same places

  • 02.09.2026Award of the landscaping (Grünordnung) works for the doctors' house (Ärztehaus) connecting to the Waldschulsiedlung area near Ritter-Tuschl-Str., within the urban development funding (Städtebauförderung) of Aldersbach.
  • 02.09.2026Replacement of the existing entrance gate at the Kläranlage Aldersbach (wastewater treatment plant) due to age, technical defects and TÜV inspection complaints.
  • 02.09.2026Presentation and approval of the draft development plan (Bebauungsplan) for the new building area „Schwaig X“, including the completed traffic study on the Abt-Zanker-Straße and the Erschließungskonzept.
  • 02.09.2026The council considered a request from the Nachbarschaftshilfe Aldersbach and the Helferkreis Aidenbach for increased financial support of the non-profit facility, including rent costs for the „Repair Cafe“ and „Kleiderkammer“.
  • 02.09.2026The council considered an application by TC Aldersbach e. V. for a subsidy to convert the four existing Ziegelmehlplätze (clay courts) into year-round all-weather courts, with a total investment volume of around 300,000 Euro.
  • 02.09.2026Due to recurring vandalism incidents in Aldersbach, the council considered the introduction of a Sicherheitswacht (volunteer security watch) to support the police.
  • 29.07.2026The municipality of Aldersbach is having its homepage completely renewed due to outdated security standards and legal accessibility requirements, awarded to the Cosmema GmbH from Gaimersheim, including a new structure, updated content, social media and app integration, and an AI-based virtual town hall assistant.
  • 29.07.2026The building application (Bauantrag) by Josef Buchbauer for the construction of two commercial halls (Gewerbehallen) in Aldersbach.
