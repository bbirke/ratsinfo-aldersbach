TOP 7Council meeting Wednesday, 10 September 2025

The Regional Planning Association (Regionale Planungsverband) has designated a priority area for wind energy installations (Vorranggebiet für Windenergieanlagen) east of Haidenburg.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The Gemeinderat consented ("stimmte zu") to the priority area under conditions: no impairment of residents, consideration of nature and noise protection, and addition of an energy storage facility for grid stabilization.
