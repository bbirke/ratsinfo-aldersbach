TOP 10-11Council meeting Thursday, 21 May 2026

The council considered two building applications (Bauanträge) by Walter Veitweber in Gumperting: conversion and extension of an open-air pig barn for animal welfare improvement without increasing the stock, and construction of a straw storage hall.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council approved ("stimmte zu") both building applications.
