TOP 04Council meeting Wednesday, 28 January 2026

The council considered an application by the citizens of Haidenburg for inclusion in the Dorferneuerungsprogramm (village renewal programme).

Befürwortung · what was decided

The council favoured ("befürwortete") the citizens' request and commissioned the municipal administration to submit the application to the 'Amt für ländliche Entwicklung Landau'.
