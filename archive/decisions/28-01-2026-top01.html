TOP 01Council meeting Wednesday, 28 January 2026

Extension of the footpath along the Knorr-Bremse company premises in Aldersbach, from the rainwater retention basin to the gatehouse, by approximately 400 meters.

Zustimmung · what was decided

The council approved ("stimmte zu") the construction measure and favoured ("befürwortet") splitting the actual total costs between Knorr-Bremse and the municipality of Aldersbach.

  • TOP 01Extension of the footpath along the Knorr-Bremse company premises in Aldersbach, from the rainwater retention basin to the gatehouse, by approximately 400 meters.

  • TOP 03The council considered a building/sanitation advisory service required under the intermunicipal funding programme for inner development of the ILE Klosterwinkel.
  • TOP 04The council considered an application by the citizens of Haidenburg for inclusion in the Dorferneuerungsprogramm (village renewal programme).
  • TOP 05-07The council considered several building applications (Bauanträge), including the installation of two [items] by Stefan and Verena Nagl, a new bungalow with garage by Julia Datzmann, and the demolition and reconstruction of a family home with garage by Dominik Bruckmeier in Aldersbach.
  • TOP 08Second application by BRG Donau-Wald GmbH & Co. KG for a modification permit (Änderungsgenehmigung) to operate a construction rubble recycling plant at the Deponie Hiendlöd site.
  • TOP 09The council considered a request from the libraries (Büchereien) in the municipality of Aldersbach to increase the annual municipal subsidies for the libraries in Aldersbach, Uttigkofen, Walchsing and Pörndorf.
  • TOP 10The Sports Club Sportfreunde Kriestorf e. V. applied for a subsidy for the purchase of a robotic lawn mower.
  • TOP 11The council discussed the initiative „Familienfreundliche Gemeinde“, which grants a discount to buyers of building plots with children in certain Baugebiete, and considered adjusting the guidelines to allow partial funding outside those areas.
