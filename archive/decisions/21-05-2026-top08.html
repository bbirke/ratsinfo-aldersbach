TOP 08Council meeting Thursday, 21 May 2026

The council addressed the near-full occupancy of the urn graves (Urnengräber) at the Friedhof St. Peter in Aldersbach and the planned erection of new urn walls (Urnenwände) to expand urn burial capacity.

Vergabe · what was decided

The council awarded ("vergab") the planning services for the urn wall expansion to the Ingenieurbüro Straubinger.
