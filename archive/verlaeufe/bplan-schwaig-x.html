Bebauungsplan "Schwaig X" (new residential area)

open

2. öffentliche Auslegung läuft (Bekanntmachung vom 20.10.2026); Wasserrechtsverfahren wird vorbereitet

From the Aufstellungsbeschluss (resolution to draw up the plan, 20.04.2022) via preliminary draft, first public Auslegung (2025), Abwägung (weighing of objections) to the second Auslegung and the Satzung (statute) in autumn 2026. Traffic study and noise report accompany the process.

Council decisions over time (5)

  1. 29.01.2025 TOP 06BeschlussThe council dealt with the planned new building area „Schwaig X“ at the western edge of the village, following the public and authority participation (Beteiligung) held from 22 November to 23 December 2024.
  2. 25.06.2025 TOP 05BeschlussChanges in the development (Erschließung) of the building area „Schwaig X“ require a revision of the Bebauungsplan, including public infiltration swales for rainwater disposal.
  3. 10.09.2025 TOP 9KenntnisnahmeThe council discussed the suitability of five inner-village sites in Aldersbach for a senior housing project („Seniorenwohnen“) examined by the commissioned architecture firm Arc.
  4. 24.06.2026 TOP 02KenntnisnahmeDue to objections from residents regarding the development of the Baugebiet „Schwaig X“, the administration, together with the engineering office, obtained a quote for a traffic study (Verkehrsuntersuchung) covering the Abt-Zanker-Straße and the Baugebiet Schwaig X.
  5. 02.09.2026 TOP 04BilligungPresentation and approval of the draft development plan (Bebauungsplan) for the new building area „Schwaig X“, including the completed traffic study on the Abt-Zanker-Straße and the Erschließungskonzept.
