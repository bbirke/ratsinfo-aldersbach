From Klosterkurier to a council information system (Heimat-Info / Session.Net)

open

Session.Net beschlossen (29.07.2026); Ratsinformationssystem noch nicht online

Aldersbach had no council information system (Ratsinformationssystem). In June 2026 the council introduced the Heimat-Info citizen app; on 29.07.2026 it decided to buy the AKDB add-on "Session.Net". Once it goes online, this archive can switch to official minutes.

Council decisions over time (4)

  1. 25.02.2026 TOP 1BeschlussThe council considered the creation of a new municipal homepage for the Gemeinde Aldersbach, as the existing homepage no longer meets current security standards and legal requirements. The award of the services „Erstellung und Neugestaltung“ (creation and redesign) was discussed.
  2. 24.06.2026 TOP 03BeschlussIntroduction of the Heimat-Info App for the municipality of Aldersbach as a supplement to the homepage, including free public relations opportunities for local clubs.
  3. 29.07.2026 TOP 01ZustimmungAward of a contract to AKDB for the Ratsinformationssystem add-on module „Session.Net“ (council information system), which complements the existing meeting management software „Session“.
  4. 29.07.2026 TOP 06KenntnisnahmeThe municipality of Aldersbach is having its homepage completely renewed due to outdated security standards and legal accessibility requirements, awarded to the Cosmema GmbH from Gaimersheim, including a new structure, updated content, social media and app integration, and an AI-based virtual town hall assistant.
