Glasfaser / Breitband (fibre-optic broadband expansion)

open

Förderantrag nach Gigabit-Richtlinie gestellt; Auswahlverfahren des Netzbetreibers ausstehend

Fibre-optic rollout thread: a market exploration (15.04. to 13.06.2025) found no provider willing to build commercially, so the council had a federal Gigabit-Richtlinie funding application submitted and, conditional on approval, decided on a selection procedure for a network operator (30.07.2025). Several 2025 street-rehabilitation works were deferred where broadband ducts will be laid first (10.09.2025). In Walchsing, the 2022 Leonet network switches operator to "Unsere Grüne Glasfaser" (UGG) with a last free house connection until 30.06.2026 (21.05.2026). Open thread: the outcome of the funding application and operator selection had not been reported by 02.09.2026; the next update is expected in issue 4/2026 (10 December 2026).

Council decisions over time (3)

  1. 30.07.2025 TOP 13BeschlussThe council dealt with broadband (Glasfaserausbau) expansion in Aldersbach, where a market exploration procedure from 15 April to 13 June 2025 found no net provider willing to expand fiber commercially. The municipality will submit a funding application under the federal Gigabit funding directive and, if approved, conduct a selection procedure for a network operator.
  2. 10.09.2025 TOP 4ZustimmungThe municipal council considered the tender for the road rehabilitation works planned for 2025, prepared by Ingenieurbüro Straubinger based on inspections by the engineer, administration and Bauhof.
  3. 21.05.2026 TOP 12KenntnisnahmeInformation on current developments in the fiber-optic (Glasfaserausbau) expansion in the Ortsteil Walchsing, including the change of network operator to „Unsere Grüne Glasfaser“ (UGG) and a final opportunity for a free house connection.
