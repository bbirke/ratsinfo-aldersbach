Water, sewage and the new split sewage fee (getrennte Abwassergebühr)

open

laufendes Aufgabengebiet: Pumpstationen, Gebührenmodelle, Kläranlage-Vereinbarung mit Aidenbach

Pump stations, sewage treatment plant agreement with Markt Aidenbach, and the introduction of the split sewage fee (GAG) including the informational brochure.

Council decisions over time (13)

  1. 30.07.2025 TOP 02ZustimmungIntroduction of the split wastewater fee („gesplittete Abwassergebühr“) in Aldersbach, requiring a new software module and support from Ingenieurbüro S² for the transition.
  2. 16.10.2025 TOP 01BeschlussThe council addressed the replacement of the two old centrifugal pumps (Kreiselpumpen) at the Pumpstation Freundorf.
  3. 16.10.2025 TOP 02KenntnisnahmeSanierung der technischen Maschinenausrüstung und Einbauten an vier gemeindlichen Pumpstationen in Aldersbach-Kollberg, Walchsing, Kriestorf und Pörndorf.
  4. 16.10.2025 TOP 03VergabeRenewal of the electronic equipment of the four pump stations (continuation of TOP 02).
  5. 11.12.2025 TOP 09ZustimmungThe council addressed the immission control partial permitting procedure II (immissionsschutzrechtliches Teilgenehmigungsverfahren II) of the company Knorr-Bremse SfN GmbH, covering the construction and operation of a KTL-Anlage (cathodic dip painting), a wastewater facility, an anodizing facility, and the operation of a paint and chemical storage.
  6. 11.12.2025 TOP 11KenntnisnahmeRecalculation of the Herstellungsbeiträge (development connection fees) for the Entwässerungseinrichtung (drainage facility) based on the municipal auditor's report, with new rates per square metre of property and floor area.
  7. 11.12.2025 TOP 12KenntnisnahmeRecalculation of the connection fees (Herstellungsbeiträge) for the municipal water supply, as required by the Rechnungsprüfungsamt, with new rates per square metre of Grundstücksfläche and Geschoßfläche.
  8. 25.02.2026 TOP 7VertagungThe item concerns an application by the Kreisgruppe Vilshofen for permission to set up and operate a container for a Konfiskatkühlung (confiscated-material cooling) at the Kläranlage (wastewater treatment plant) in Aldersbach.
  9. 29.04.2026 TOP 11AblehnungThe council again considered an application for the installation and operation of a container for confiscated-goods cooling (Konfiskatkühlung) at the wastewater treatment plant or on the site of the sewage sludge drying facility.
  10. 24.06.2026 TOP 14KenntnisnahmeThe item concerns the wastewater treatment plant (Kläranlage) Aldersbach and the second amendment of the Zweckvereinbarung (mutual agreement) between the Gemeinde Aldersbach and the Markt Aidenbach on joint wastewater disposal, including an adjustment of the cost allocation key for operating, maintenance and investment costs.
  11. 29.07.2026 TOP 02BeschlussReplacement of the outdated remote control technology at the Drucksteigerungspumpwerk Wifling (booster pump station), which caused repeated failures and data transmission faults to the municipal water supply's process control system.
  12. 29.07.2026 TOP 11BeschlussThe council addressed the initiation of a Bebauungs- und Grünordnungsplan (development and green structure plan) for the area Haidenburg/Häusling, including a general residential area and a special area for a Schützenheim (marksmen's clubhouse).
  13. 02.09.2026 TOP 03BeschlussReplacement of the existing entrance gate at the Kläranlage Aldersbach (wastewater treatment plant) due to age, technical defects and TÜV inspection complaints.
