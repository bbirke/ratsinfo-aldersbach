Vorhabenbezogener Bebauungsplan "SO Energie - Batteriespeicheranlage am Umspannwerk"

open

Aufstellungsbeschluss gefasst (11.12.2025); öffentliche Auslegung und Festsetzung stehen aus

Grid-scale battery storage near the Pörndorf substation: Gestattungsvertrag (grid connection consent), Aufstellungsbeschluss with parallel F-Plan change (Deckblatt Nr. 40), early public participation.

Council decisions over time (9)

  1. 19.02.2025 TOP 03-05ZustimmungThe council dealt with the planned construction of a battery storage facility (Batteriespeicheranlage) on a plot of approx. 8000 m² southwest of Pörndorf, including the preliminary Bebauungsplan draft 'SO Energie - Batteriespeicheranlage am Umspannwerk' and the amendment of the Flächennutzungsplan.
  2. 17.03.2025 TOP 03-04ZustimmungThe council considered two building applications (Bauanträge): Andreas Wagner's new Agri-PV system with battery storage in Freundorf and Gisela Seidenberger's renovation of an existing single-family house with garage in Pörndorf.
  3. 19.05.2025 TOP 03BefürwortungThe municipality of Aldersbach was involved in the land-use planning (Bauleitplanung) of a battery storage facility for renewable energies in the municipality of Roßbach, concerning the planned amendment of the development plan "Industrie- und Gewerbegebiet Esterndorf".
  4. 25.06.2025 TOP 07-08ZustimmungThe council dealt with the vorhabenbezogener Bebauungsplan (project-related development plan) „SO Energie - Batteriespeicheranlage am Umspannwerk“ for a battery storage facility in Pörndorf, including the parallel amendment of the Flächennutzungs- und Landschaftsplan by Deckblatt Nr. 35.
  5. 30.07.2025 TOP 12KenntnisnahmeNotification that the Anhörungsverfahren (hearing procedure) for the Planfeststellungsverfahren (plan approval procedure) for the new 380-kV power line Prienbach - Pleinting by TenneT TSO GmbH has been initiated, including the route options „Trasse 2A“ and „Trasse 2B“.
  6. 16.10.2025 TOP 07-08KenntnisnahmeEstablishment of the vorhabenbezogener Bebauungsplan (project-related development plan) „SO Energie - Batteriespeicheranlage am Umspannwerk“ and amendment of the Flächennutzungs- und Landschaftsplan (land use and landscape plan) via Deckblatt Nr. 35 for a battery storage facility in Pörndorf.
  7. 16.10.2025 TOP 09ZustimmungThe council dealt with the Bauleitplanverfahren (urban land-use planning procedure) for the construction of an Energie-Batteriespeicheranlage (battery storage facility) at the Umspannwerk in Aldersbach.
  8. 11.12.2025 TOP 02-03BeschlussThe council dealt with the amendment of the Flächennutzungs- und Landschaftsplan (regional land-use and landscape plan) by Deckblatt Nr. 40 and the establishment of the vorhabenbezogener Bebauungs- und Grünordnungsplan „SO Energie-Batteriespeicheranlage am Umspannwerk/Bayernwerk Natur“ (project-related development and green structure plan for a battery storage facility).
  9. 25.02.2026 TOP 15KenntnisnahmeNotification of building projects exempt from approval procedures (Genehmigungsfreistellungsverfahren): a battery storage facility by Photo Volta Batteriespeicher Aldersbach GmbH in the Bebauungsplan „SO Energie- und Batteriespeicheranlage am Umspannwerk“ and a production hall by HU-Immo GmbH in the Bebauungsplan „Gewerbegebiet Uttigkofen“.
