Städtebauförderung Ortskern Aldersbach (urban development around Zisterzienserstraße)

open

2. Bauabschnitt: Vorentwurf gebilligt (29.07.2026), Förderantrag bei der Regierung von Niederbayern läuft

Urban-renewal programme in the town centre: parking Brauerei (1st construction phase), then planning of the 2nd phase along Zisterzienserstraße.

Council decisions over time (10)

  1. 29.01.2025 TOP 07ZustimmungThe council dealt with the planned new construction of the Bauhof (municipal yard) including an existing building and the demolition of two halls on the former brewery site, which requires a change to the Bebauungsplan „Ortskern Aldersbach“ (local development plan). The public display of the plan had taken place from 22 November to 23 December 2024.
  2. 29.01.2025 TOP 08BeschlussAmendment of the Flächennutzungs- und Landschaftsplan (land use and landscape plan) and of the Bebauungsplan „Ortskern Aldersbach“ (local development plan), including changing the designated area type to 'Sondergebiet' for a new municipal Bauhof with a multi-purpose hall and the designation of the existing Volksfestplatz.
  3. 10.09.2025 TOP 3BeschlussErection of a digital information and advertising display at the Brauereiparkplatz as part of urban development funding (Städtebauförderung), with the contract awarded to the firm Rotapanel (Netherlands).
  4. 16.10.2025 TOP 10-11BeschlussThe council dealt with the planned amendment of the Bebauungsplan „Ortskern Aldersbach“ Deckblatt Nr. 15 and the corresponding amendment of the Flächennutzungsplan through Deckblatt Nr. 34, following renewed public participation.
  5. 12.11.2025 TOP 17ZustimmungPresentation of the draft planning for the 2nd construction phase of the urban development funding (Städtebauförderung) in Aldersbach, concerning the reorganization along the Zisterzienserstraße following completion of the 1st construction phase (parking lot Brauerei with surroundings).
  6. 11.12.2025 TOP 05EinvernehmenThe council dealt with a Bauvoranfrage (preliminary building enquiry) by Dr. Nikolaus Arndt for the construction of a Seniorenwohnanlage (senior housing facility) in Aldersbach, Ortsmitte near Dorfanger, within the Bebauungsplan „Ortskern Aldersbach“.
  7. 29.04.2026 TOP 01KenntnisnahmeThe council discussed the redesign of the eastern village entrance (BA III Baulos), including a footpath connection to the Ärztehaus via a footbridge structure to the Ritter-Tuschl-Straße.
  8. 29.04.2026 TOP 02VergabeThe municipal council awarded the steel construction contract (Stahlbau) for a measure funded under the Städtebauförderung to the firm Pichler GmbH, following the recommendation of the engineering office Schönbuchner.
  9. 21.05.2026 TOP 07ZustimmungLighting of the Stegbauwerk (footbridge structure) connecting the Ärztehaus to the Staatsstraße in the area of the Robert-Daiser-Straße as part of urban development funding.
  10. 02.09.2026 TOP 02KenntnisnahmeAward of the landscaping (Grünordnung) works for the doctors' house (Ärztehaus) connecting to the Waldschulsiedlung area near Ritter-Tuschl-Str., within the urban development funding (Städtebauförderung) of Aldersbach.
