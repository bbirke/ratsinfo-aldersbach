Glossary

German council terms, explained

The archive keeps German legal and planning terms as printed. Here is what they mean.

Decision kinds

Every agenda item gets one badge, derived from the vote phrase the newsletter prints. If the text names no decision, the archive says so.

Beschluss decision
The council passed a formal resolution. The newsletter says "beschloss", "beschließt", "entschied" or similar. (47 items)
Vergabe contract award
The council awarded a contract (works, supplies or services) to a company. (11 items)
Zustimmung consent / approval
The council agreed to or approved a proposal, application or request. (81 items)
Billigung assent
The council approved a draft (typically a plan draft) so that the procedure can continue. Not yet the final decision. (9 items)
Einvernehmen statutory consent
Gemeindliches Einvernehmen: the municipality's statutory consent to a building project that the district office (Landratsamt) decides on (section 36 BauGB). (2 items)
Befürwortung endorsement
The council spoke in favour of something that another body decides. (16 items)
Bestätigung confirmation
The council confirmed an earlier decision, an election result or an appointment. (4 items)
Ablehnung rejection
The council rejected the proposal or application. (5 items)
Vertagung deferred
The item was postponed to a later meeting. (3 items)
Kenntnisnahme taken note, no decision
The council was informed and took note. No decision was taken. (37 items)
Kein Beschluss genannt no decision stated
The newsletter text does not say what (if anything) was decided. The archive does not guess. (0 items)

Terms

Gemeinderat municipal council
The elected council of the municipality, chaired by the first mayor (Erster Bürgermeister).
TOP (Tagesordnungspunkt) agenda item
One numbered item of a council meeting. TOP numbers are taken from the newsletter as printed.
Gemeinderatssitzung council meeting
A meeting of the council. The newsletter reports the public part under "Infos aus der Gemeinderatssitzung".
Aldersbacher Klosterkurier municipal newsletter
The quarterly official newsletter of Aldersbach and the primary source of this archive.
Niederschrift minutes
The official record of a meeting. Only a few extracts (Auszüge) are published online.
Bebauungsplan (B-Plan) development plan
Legally binding plan that sets what may be built on which plots in a defined area.
Vorhabenbezogener Bebauungsplan project-based development plan
A development plan drawn up together with a specific investor and project.
Flächennutzungsplan (F-Plan, FNP) land-use plan
Preparatory plan for the whole municipality showing intended land uses. Changes are made via numbered Deckblätter.
Deckblatt amendment sheet
A numbered amendment to the Flächennutzungsplan or a Bebauungsplan.
Aufstellungsbeschluss resolution to draw up a plan
The council's formal start of a planning procedure.
Vorentwurf / Entwurf preliminary draft / draft
Draft versions of a plan that are put out for participation.
Öffentliche Auslegung public display
The plan draft is made available to the public and authorities, who can submit comments within a deadline.
Abwägung weighing of comments
The council's formal assessment of every comment received during participation, with a decision on how each is handled.
Satzung / Satzungsbeschluss statute / adoption
A by-law of the municipality. Adopting a development plan as Satzung makes it legally binding.
Bauantrag / Bauvoranfrage building application / preliminary enquiry
Applications to build. The council gives or refuses its Einvernehmen; the district office decides.
Flurstück, Fl.Nr., Gemarkung parcel, parcel number, cadastral district
Cadastral identifiers of a plot of land. Parcel pins on the maps are resolved via BayernAtlas (ALKIS).
Städtebauförderung urban development funding
State and federal funding programme for town-centre renewal.
Ratsinformationssystem council information system
Online system for agendas and minutes. Aldersbach decided to introduce AKDB "Session.Net" on 29.07.2026.

Ratsinfo-Archiv Aldersbach

An independent citizen archive of the decisions of the Gemeinderat Aldersbach (Landkreis Passau), reconstructed from the municipal newsletter "Aldersbacher Klosterkurier" and documents from aldersbach.de. Not an official publication. English summaries; German source text always verbatim.
